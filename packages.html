FAQ · Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about hosting, revisions, payment security, and custom projects.

What if I don't have a domain or hosting yet? +

We provide free consultation and recommendations based on your budget and tech needs (e.g. free modern hosting on Vercel / Netlify / Cloudflare or Thai business hosting). We also handle full DNS setup and deployment until your site is 100% live.

How many revision rounds are included, and what is the scope? +

Revisions follow the designated rounds of each package (Basic: 2 rounds, Standard: 3 rounds, Premium: unlimited within original scope). Revisions cover styling adjustments, layout tuning, and text/asset updates. Any minor bug fixes or typos are always free and do not count against your revision rounds.

Can I request custom features outside the 3 packages? +

Absolutely! Feel free to chat with us to describe your custom requirements or share reference websites. We will assess the actual scope and issue a custom quote directly on Fastwork tailored to your budget and needs.

How does payment work? Is it secure? +

All payments are securely processed through Fastwork's Escrow system. Your payment is held safely by the platform and only transferred to the freelancer after you inspect and click to approve final delivery. 100% safe and scam-free.

How is the project delivered, and what files do I receive? +

We deliver the complete, clean source code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript or Next.js / React project) along with all design assets. You own 100% of the code. We also provide initial deployment guidance and support to ensure your website runs smoothly.
