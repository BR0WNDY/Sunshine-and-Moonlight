# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, collaborators, and peers. Their context is evaluating technical capabilities, looking for passion in coding, and seeking potential candidates for jobs or projects. The job to be done is to understand Suwapat's technical skills, logical approach, and personality.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio ("Projects Sunshine and Moonlights") to showcase coding projects (like the Sorting Hat and Thai Govt Calculator), share knowledge, and secure jobs or collaborations while showing a deep passion for Computer Science.

## Brand Personality
Intellectual, Magical, Professional. The tone should reflect a "Ravenclaw" identity: wisdom, wit, and a logical approach to problem-solving, mixed with a sense of wonder.

## Anti-references
Generic corporate templates, dry and emotionless technical portfolios, or cluttered layouts. It should not look like a generic SaaS landing page.

## Design Principles
- "Show, don't tell": Demonstrate technical logic and coding skills through interactive or visually engaging projects.
- "Intellectual magic": Blend structured, high-quality code with a sense of wonder and creativity.
- "Precision and clarity": Keep layouts clean, well-structured, and thoughtful, reflecting deep analysis before execution.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance, clear typography for readability, and accessible contrast to ensure the portfolio is usable by everyone, including recruiters with different viewing needs.
