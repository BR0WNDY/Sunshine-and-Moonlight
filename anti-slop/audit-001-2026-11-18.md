## Findings List

### 🔴 HIGH Priority (Hard Gate Rules)

1. **[HIGH] R-03 — Mobile Tap Targets Below 44px Minimum**  
   *Reason:* Terminal chips (`.term-chips .chip` at `min-height: 34px` in `index.html:198`), language toggle buttons (`.lang-btn` at `padding: 6px 11px` in `assets/styles.css:62`), and `.demo-link` buttons (`packages.html:46`) are smaller than the 44×44px minimum touch target on mobile screens.

2. **[HIGH] R-32 — Missing Focus Indicator on Input Controls**  
   *Reason:* `paotang.html:147` (`#customQuota` inline `outline:none`) and `index.html:181` (`.term-input input:focus-visible{outline:0}`) remove the native focus ring without providing a visible `:focus` or `:focus-within` replacement indicator for keyboard users.

3. **[HIGH] R-39 — Missing Base `cursor: pointer` & `:disabled` Affordances + Infinite Motion**  
   *Reason:* Neither `index.html` nor `assets/styles.css` defines a base CSS rule setting `cursor: pointer` on all enabled interactive controls (`a[href], button, summary, select, label[for]`) and `cursor: not-allowed` on `:disabled` controls, and `.ring` (`spin 48s infinite`) / `.stars` (`twinkle 6s infinite`) run continuously longer than 5 seconds without a pause control.

4. **[HIGH] R-25 — Low Color Contrast on Muted Monospace Labels & Secondary Text**  
   *Reason:* `--muted` (`#9db0d4` or `#8b9bb4` at `0.68rem–0.74rem` uppercase on `rgba(16,31,66,.85)` or `--night-2`) and `.term .cm` / `.term .ps` can dip near or below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast threshold for small text.

5. **[HIGH] R-36 / R-40 — Unmeasured Performance Number Claim in Scope Matrix**  
   *Reason:* `packages.html` Scope Matrix claims `< 2s Load Time` (`โหลดไว < 2 วินาที`) as a blanket guarantee without a measured field benchmark; should be stated honestly as optimized static code & asset compression rather than an unmeasured numeric SLA.

6. **[HIGH] R-02 — Em Dash (`—`) Overuse Across UI Copy**  
   *Reason:* There are 78 occurrences of the em dash character (`—`) across `index.html` (59), `sortinghats.html` (8), `packages.html` (5), `paotang.html` (3), `work.html` (2), and `assets/styles.css` (1); Note: per R-37 conflict protocol, if you prefer to keep em dashes as part of your personal editorial voice, you may skip `#6` or approve replacing them with colons/commas/periods.

### 🟡 MEDIUM Priority (Purpose-Gate Rules)

7. **[MEDIUM] R-10 — Excessive Glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur`) on `index.html`**  
   *Reason:* `index.html` applies `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` to 6 different surfaces simultaneously (header nav, badge card, modal backdrop, etc.), exceeding the R-10 dose cap of 1–2 accented surfaces and flattening depth hierarchy.

8. **[MEDIUM] R-07 — Decorative Background Grid Stacked Over Starfield (`index.html`)**  
   *Reason:* `<div class="grid-bg">` (`index.html:809`) layers a generic CSS blueprint grid over `.stars` and `.glow`, stacking three background textures at once when the Ravenclaw starfield alone carries the brand motif.

9. **[MEDIUM] R-08 — Decorative Arrows (`→`) on Nearly Every Button**  
   *Reason:* Almost every primary and ghost CTA across `index.html`, `packages.html`, and `work.html` appends a trailing `→` arrow by default rather than reserving directional arrows for navigation/external links where they communicate distinct intent.

10. **[MEDIUM] R-14 — Back-to-Back Identical 4-Column Card Grids (`packages.html` & `work.html`)**  
    *Reason:* `.flow-grid` (4 cards) is immediately followed by `.prep-grid` (4 cards of identical width and rhythm); converting the Preparation Checklist into a compact 2-column checklist panel or structured 2×2 split creates clear visual hierarchy between the "How We Work" timeline and "What to Prepare".

---

### 🟢 LOW Priority (Quality Locks & Visual Polish)

11. **[LOW] R-05 / R-20 — Uniform Section Header Rhythm Across Subpages**  
    *Reason:* Every section in `packages.html` and `work.html` repeats the exact same `h2.sec-title` + `p.sec-sub` + card-grid stack; adding structural rhythm (e.g., side-by-side sticky intro on the FAQ section or an asymmetric split for the Preparation section) will make the page feel editorial rather than template-driven.
